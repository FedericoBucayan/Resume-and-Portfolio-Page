Setup Instructions

Step 1 Paste & Save in Gmail

  1. Enter your GitHub URL in the "Profile Picture Image URL" card on the left.
  2. Click the **Copy Signature (Rich Text)** button on the preview card.
  3. Open your web browser and navigate to **Gmail**.
  4. Click the gear icon (Settings) in the top-right corner, then click **See all settings**.
  5. Scroll down to the **Signature** section.
  6. Click **Create new** (or select your current one).
  7. Click inside the signature editor textbox and press **Ctrl+V** (or **Cmd+V** on Mac) to paste.
  8. Confirm the profile picture appears loaded.
  9. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Save Changes**.

Step 2 Outlook Web / Outlook.com

  1. Click the **Copy Signature** button.
  2. Go to Outlook.com settings (gear icon) → **View all Outlook settings** → **Compose and reply**.
  3. Under **Email signature**, paste (**Ctrl+V**) into the text box.
  4. Click **Save**.
